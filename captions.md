## Day 4 - Closing your oldest card (carousel, 7 slides)
Closing your oldest card can cost you more than you think. 🏛️

It can shrink your total limit, which can push your utilization up. Keep it open, put one small recurring charge on it, and set autopay.

Annual fee? Ask about a no-fee version first.

## Day 5 - Autopay (carousel, 6 slides)
The easiest foundation upgrade in credit. ✅

Turn on autopay for the minimum so you never miss a due date. Then pay the rest yourself before the statement closes. Keep a buffer in the account so a payment never bounces.

Save this and follow for one blueprint a day.

## Day 6 - Report vs score (carousel, 6 slides)
Your credit report is the record. Your credit score is the math on it. 📋

Check your reports for errors. Free reports are available at AnnualCreditReport.com. If something is wrong, dispute it with the bureau that shows it.
