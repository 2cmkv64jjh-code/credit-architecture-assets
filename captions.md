## Day 4 - Closing your oldest card (carousel, 7 slides)
Closing your oldest card can cost you more than you think. 🏛️

It can shrink your total limit, which can push your utilization up. Keep it open, put one small recurring charge on it, and set autopay.

Annual fee? Ask about a no-fee version first.

## Day 5 - Autopay (carousel, 6 slides)
The easiest foundation upgrade in credit. ✅

Turn on autopay for the minimum so you never miss a due date. Then pay the rest yourself before the statement closes. Keep a buffer in the account so a payment never bounces.

## Day 8 - Hard vs soft inquiries (7 slides)
Hard inquiry vs soft inquiry. 🔍

A hard inquiry happens when you apply for credit and can lower your score by a few points. A soft inquiry, like checking your own credit or a pre-qualification, doesn't affect it. Shopping for one loan? Keep your applications in a short window.

## Day 9 - Secured cards (7 slides)
How a secured card builds credit. 🔐

You put down a deposit, which usually becomes your limit. Make sure the card reports to all three bureaus, keep the balance low, and pay on time. Ask if it can graduate to an unsecured card.

## Day 10 - Credit-builder loans (7 slides)
Credit-builder loans: borrow money you can't touch yet. 🧱

The lender holds the funds while you make payments. It only builds credit if the lender reports to the bureaus, so ask before you sign, and compare the total cost.

## Day 11 - Authorized user (7 slides)
Becoming an authorized user: the upside and the risk. 🤝

Their well-managed account can show up on your report. Their late payments and high balances can too. Choose someone with a strong history and ask if the issuer reports authorized users.

How to dispute a credit report error. 🛠️

Pull your reports at AnnualCreditReport.com, mark the error, file with the bureau that shows it, and include proof. Keep copies of everything and check the result.
