It can shrink your total limit, which can push your utilization up. Keep it open, put one small recurring charge on it, and set autopay.

## Day 8 - Hard vs soft inquiries (7 slides)
Hard inquiry vs soft inquiry. 🔍

A hard inquiry happens when you apply for credit and can lower your score by a few points. A soft inquiry, like checking your own credit or a pre-qualification, doesn't affect it. Shopping for one loan? Keep your applications in a short window.

## Day 15
Credit freeze vs fraud alert. 🛡️

A freeze locks your credit file and is free. A fraud alert asks lenders to verify it is you, is free, and lasts one year. An extended alert can last seven years if you have an identity theft report.

A freeze goes through each bureau. A fraud alert goes through one.

Save this and follow for one blueprint a day.

#credit #creditfreeze #fraudalert #identitytheft #creditbuilding #personalfinance #financialliteracy

## Day 16
Statement date vs due date. 📅

The statement date is when your cycle closes, and the balance on that date is typically what gets reported. The due date is your payment deadline. Pay down before the statement closes, then pay the rest by the due date.

Score ranges. 🎯

FICO scores typically run from 300 to 850. Bands: 800+ exceptional, 740-799 very good, 670-739 good, 580-669 fair, below 580 poor. Lenders set their own cutoffs, so treat these as a guide.
